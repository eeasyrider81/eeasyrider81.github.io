Willow privacy policy

Willow is a private, home-made desk robot used only by its owner. It is not a public service.

What Willow accesses

Only the owner's own Google Calendar, and only after the owner has allowed it. Willow reads events to answer the owner's questions and to give reminders, and adds, moves or deletes events only when the owner asks and confirms.

Where the data goes

Calendar data is used on the owner's own computer at home. Reminders of upcoming events (the event's name and time) are sent to the owner's own phone through a notification service (ntfy.sh). Calendar data is not sold, shared with other people, or used for advertising.

Stopping access

The owner can remove Willow's access at any time in their Google Account under "Security", "Third-party apps with account access".
